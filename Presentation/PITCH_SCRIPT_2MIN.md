# LocoCisco — 2-Minute Pitch Script

> A tight run through all 11 slides in ~2 minutes. Story only, no details — let
> the demo carry the "how." ~230 words; at a normal pace this lands around
> 1:45–2:00. Bracketed cues tell you when to advance.

---

**[Slide 1 — Title]**
Hi — we're LocoCisco. A local AI voice system that runs entirely on your own
hardware.

**[Slide 2 — Team]**
We're a small team across edge systems, speech ML, product, and security.

**[Slide 3 — Problem]**
Voice is the most natural interface, but today's voice AI is cloud-bound. It
ships your audio off-site — in hotels, clinics, agencies, police stations, where
it can't go — it's slow and breaks without connectivity, and most of what's said
is never captured into action.

**[Slide 4 — Solution]**
LocoCisco listens without a wake word, understands who said what, and turns
speech into action — tasks, decisions, commands — all running locally, with
nothing sent to the cloud. Captions, translation, agents: one system, many jobs.

**[Slide 5 — How it works]**
It captures the room, attributes the speech, decides with local models, and runs
a policy gate before anything is sent out. No cloud round-trip. Works offline.

**[Slide 6 — Near-term market]**
Near term, that fits every business and government location where people talk and
decisions happen — hospitality, restaurants, customer service, government, and
police.

**[Slide 7 — Vision]**
Long term, the same local AI belongs in every home — assistants that understand
you without streaming your private audio to big tech.

**[Slide 8 — Architecture]**
The architecture is simple: one edge server runs the models; thin clients sit in
every room, desk, or vehicle. Adding a location is adding a client — not another
AI deployment.

**[Slide 9 — Edge vs cloud]**
Deploy on edge or cloud. Edge when confidentiality is critical; cloud when ease
of management wins. Same product — your call.

**[Slide 10 — Roadmap & ask]**
We're ready to pilot: one edge server, a handful of clients, one vertical —
measured on real hardware.

**[Slide 11 — Contact]**
That's LocoCisco: every voice understood, nothing leaves the room. Let's talk.

---

### Delivery notes

- **Pace:** ~2 seconds between slides; don't read the bullets — say the line, advance.
- **If you're tight on time,** drop slides 2 and 7 to the spoken half-sentence and
  you'll finish under 1:45.
- **Hand-off to the demo:** end slide 5 or 10 with "…let me show you," then play
  `Demo.mp4`.
