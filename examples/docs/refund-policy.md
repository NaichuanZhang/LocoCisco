# Refund policy (customer-facing, v3)

Northwind Robotics refunds any monthly subscription in full when the customer asks within 14 days of the charge.
Annual subscriptions are refunded pro rata for the unused months when the customer cancels within 30 days;
after 30 days, annual plans are not refunded but can be converted to credit.

Hardware (the Nimbus desk robot) can be returned within 30 days of delivery for a full refund if it is undamaged.
Return shipping is free in the US, Canada and the EU.

Support handles refunds through the billing console; refunds over $5,000 need a manager's approval. The policy
owner is Lin (finance). Last updated March 2026.
