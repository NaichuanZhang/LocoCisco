# Q3 pricing proposal (Northwind Robotics, internal)

## Summary
The Q3 pricing proposal moves the Starter plan from $29 to $39 per seat per month and keeps the Team plan at
$79. The Enterprise plan stays quote-based. The change takes effect for new customers on August 1; existing
customers keep their price until their renewal date.

## What we decided last time
In the June pricing sync we decided to keep the Team plan price flat for the year and to test the Starter
increase in two regions first (Germany and Canada). The test ran for six weeks and churn did not move.
